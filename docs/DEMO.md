# Demo video (≤ 2 min)

**Rule one:** the phone is in **aeroplane mode, visibly, the whole time**. Keep the status bar in
frame. That icon is the most persuasive frame in the video.

**Rule two:** no narration over silence. Let it work. On-screen captions only where a jump in time
needs explaining.

Before recording: install the app to the home screen while online, open it once, and on the LEARN
home tap *Download voice input* if you want to show voice. Then switch on aeroplane mode. Clear any
old incident (open the record → *New incident*). Set the phone to light theme, brightness up.

| Time | Show | What the viewer learns |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Status bar with the aeroplane icon. Tap First Safety on the home screen. | No signal, and it opens anyway. |
| 0:06–0:14 | "What happened?" Tap red **Start the 15 minutes**. The timer starts at 15:00. | The first tap is washing, not questions. |
| 0:14–0:24 | Lock the phone. Count three seconds. Unlock: the timer has kept true time. | It survives a locked screen. |
| 0:24–0:52 | A helper taps **Back** to "A few facts": *About 10 min ago* · *Bit* · skin broken *Yes* · bleeding *Yes* · stray *No* · *Nothing* put on the wound · *Left open*. Then **Back to washing**. | The clinic's questions, answered while washing continues. |
| 0:52–0:58 | Caption: *15 minutes later*. The alarm sounds at 00:00 and the steps follow. | The full 15 minutes, done. |
| 0:58–1:05 | Caption: *At the clinic*. Tap **I'm at the clinic** in the header. | One tap, always there. |
| 1:05–1:30 | The record: time of bite and minutes since, washing started and done, animal, site, what was done, **"No turmeric, chilli, oil or other substance applied"**, the Unknowns. Scroll to *"It contains no medical assessment."* | Facts only, and the honest Unknowns. |
| 1:30–1:45 | Tap **Read aloud**; let it read the first lines. | For a clinician with busy hands, or someone who can't read English. |
| 1:45–1:55 | Tap **हिन्दी**: the labels switch with the English beside them. | Neither language is a dead end. |
| 1:55–2:00 | Hold on the aeroplane icon again. | It never needed the network. |

Optional, if there's time instead of the Hindi shot: on "What bit or scratched you?", type
*kutte ne haath pe kaata* and show the taps fill in by themselves, offline.
