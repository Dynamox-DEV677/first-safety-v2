# Real-phone test — 4 Oct (brief §12, last item)

Use the cheapest Android you can borrow. Do it **outdoors, in sunlight**. About 20 minutes, most of
it the wash timer running. Scan `first-safety-qr.png` (in this folder) or type
**first-safety.vercel.app**. Tick each box; write down anything odd and send it to Claude.

## Once, on Wi-Fi

- [ ] Open the site. In Chrome's menu, tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- [ ] Open it from the home-screen icon. It should open full screen, with no browser bar.
- [ ] Go to **Learn**. "Download voice input?" appears. Tap **Download voice input · about 70 MB**
      and wait for "Ready · works offline". (Skip this if you won't demo voice.)
- [ ] Close the app completely (swipe it away).

## Aeroplane mode on, for everything below

- [ ] Open from the home-screen icon. **"What happened?"** with two big buttons, and nothing spinning.
- [ ] Tap red **Start the 15 minutes**. The timer starts at 15:00.
- [ ] **Lock the phone for 30 seconds**, then unlock. The time is about 30 seconds lower, not frozen.
- [ ] Switch to another app for a minute, then come back. Still counting correctly.
- [ ] Tap **Back**: "A few facts" opens and the header bar keeps counting. Answer a few. Tap
      **Back to washing**.
- [ ] In bright sunlight, can you read every screen? (Brightness at about half.)
- [ ] All buttons are easy to hit with a thumb, and nothing scrolls sideways.
- [ ] At 00:00, does the phone **beep and vibrate**? (Sound on, not silent mode.)
- [ ] Tap **I'm at the clinic** in the header. The record shows the right time of bite, the "minutes
      ago", 15 min 00 s completed, and what you answered. Unanswered rows say **Unknown**.
- [ ] **Read aloud** speaks it. Try with the phone's volume up. (Some cheap phones need a Google
      voice installed: Settings → Text-to-speech.)
- [ ] **हिन्दी** switches the labels, with the English still underneath.
- [ ] **Copy**, then paste into a note: the whole record is there as plain text.
- [ ] **Share** opens the phone's share sheet.
- [ ] Voice (if downloaded): on "What bit or scratched you?", tap **Speak instead** and say "a
      street dog bit my hand". Note how many seconds it takes. Without voice: type it instead.
- [ ] **New incident** clears the record and goes back to "What happened?".

## Aeroplane mode off

- [ ] On "What bit or scratched you?", type **my neighbours labrador nipped my ankle** and tap
      **Use**. The online helper should fill in Dog and Legs or feet within about 2 seconds.

## If something fails

Write down the phone model, the Android version, the screen and what happened. Screenshots help.
Don't post anything with real health details in it.
