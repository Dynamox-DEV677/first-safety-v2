First Safety Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Applies to the First Safety Android app on Google Play (dev.kairo.firstsafety).

Short version: First Safety has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own that receives your data. Everything you type stays on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is your location, and only when you tap Find nearest hospital.

Who we are

First Safety is a free first-aid app for animal bites and rabies prevention, made by Kairo Industries in Chennai, India. Contact: kairoindustries.cor@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

These are saved only in the app's own storage on your device. We never receive them, and they are never uploaded:

  • emergency contacts and the medical details you choose to enter (for example age, blood group, allergies, vaccine doses);
  • the bite record and the doctor's report you build;
  • the vaccine schedule and reminder settings;
  • your state, theme, learning progress, scores and bookmarks;
  • the last hospital search result, kept for up to 7 days so it still works offline.

Printing, sharing or saving a report only happens when you tap the button, and goes to the app or printer you pick.

Location

First Safety asks for location permission only when you tap Find nearest hospital. You can say no: the list of hospitals and helplines for your state stays on screen either way.

If you allow it, the app sends your coordinates directly from your phone to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de, or the mirror overpass.kumi.systems if the first is busy) to look up hospitals within 10 km. The request carries no name, account or device ID. We do not receive or store your location. Those services are run by third parties and may keep ordinary server logs under their own policies.

Calls, maps and links

  • Tapping a phone number opens your phone's dialler with the number filled in. The app never places a call by itself.
  • Directions open Google Maps, and videos open on YouTube. Source links open the WHO, NCDC, ICMR or government websites. Once you leave the app, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Notifications

If you turn on vaccine reminders, the app shows them on your phone when you open it on a dose day. Adding doses to your calendar creates a calendar file that you open in your own calendar app. Nothing is sent to a server.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Reset all data, or use Clear all medical info on the medical profile. You can also clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

First Safety is meant for people aged 13 and over, and for parents and carers. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because nothing you enter leaves the phone.

Medical disclaimer

First Safety is not a medical device. It gives first-aid information taken from WHO and NCDC guidance, with sources shown in the app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. After any animal bite or scratch, go to a hospital or consult a healthcare professional as soon as possible. In an emergency in India, call 112.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
